"Do" ve "Make", Türkçede ikisi de "yapmak" olarak çevrilir, ancak İngilizce'de farklı durumlarda kullanılır. Aşağıda her iki fiilin kullanım farkları ve bol örneklerle açıklamaları yer almaktadır.  

## Kullanım Farkları  

| Fiil      | Kullanım Durumu                              | Örnek                                           |  
|-----------|----------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|  
| Do    | Görev, iş, sorumluluk veya rutin eylemler    | I need to do my homework.                  |  
| Make  | Yeni bir şey yaratmak veya üretmek           | She wants to make a cake.                  |  

| Do ile İfadeler         | Make ile İfadeler      |  
|-----------------------------|----------------------------|  
| Do the laundry              | Make a decision            |  
| Do your best                | Make an effort             |  
| Do business                 | Make a plan                |  
| Do someone a favor          | Make a phone call          |  
| Do the dishes               | Make a promise             |  
| Do the shopping             | Make time                  |  
| Do exercise                 | Make a mistake             |  

## Do - Örnek Kullanımlar  

> [!EXAMPLE]  
> I always do the dishes after dinner.  
> They did a great job on the project.  
> We should do some cleaning before guests arrive.  
> She doesn't like to do housework.  
> He does his exercises every morning.  
> Did you do your best in the exam?  
> I need to do my hair before the meeting.  
> They did nothing about the problem.  
> Don't forget to do your chores.  
> She promised to do the shopping later.  
> We are going to do a favor for our neighbor.  

## Make - Örnek Kullanımlar  

> [!EXAMPLE]  
> I will make a sandwich for lunch.  
> He made a mistake during the presentation.  
> Can you make some tea?  
> They need to make a decision soon.  
> She made a promise to call me later.  
> We need to make a plan for the weekend.  
> He makes an effort to learn new things every day.  
> I’m trying to make time for exercise.  
> Let's make a list of things to buy.  
> They made a lot of noise last night.  
> We are going to make a reservation at the restaurant.  
> She made an excuse for being late.  
> He makes friends easily.  

## Yanlış Kullanımlara Dikkat!  
Bazı durumlarda "do" ve "make" karıştırılabilir.  

> [!EXAMPLE]  
> ~~I will do a cake.~~ 🚫  
> I will make a cake.  
>  
> ~~She made her homework.~~ 🚫  
> She did her homework.  
>  
> ~~He did a noise.~~ 🚫  
> He made a noise.  
>  
> ~~We made our chores.~~ 🚫  
> We did our chores.
